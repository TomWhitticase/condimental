# The CONDIMENTAL name and brand

The **source code** of CONDIMENTAL is free software under the GNU General
Public License v3 — see [LICENSE](LICENSE). You may build it, play it, study
it, change it and share it, including your changed versions, on the terms
that licence sets.

The **name and brand are not covered by that licence.** "CONDIMENTAL", the
game's logo and wordmark, and the store artwork published with the official
releases (Steam capsules, key art, trailers) are reserved by the project's
owner. The GPL grants rights in the code, not in a trade name, and this file
is how the two are kept apart.

## What that means in practice

- **Building and playing it yourself is fine.** Clone the repository, run
  `cargo xtask`, play. That is what the source is here for.
- **Sharing a build of the unmodified game is fine**, under the GPL's terms
  (with the source, or an offer of it), as long as you do not present it as
  the official release or sell it under the CONDIMENTAL name.
- **A fork you distribute needs its own name.** If you publish a modified
  version — free or paid, on a store or anywhere else — rename it and replace
  the logo and store artwork, so nobody mistakes it for the official game. You
  may say truthfully that it is "based on CONDIMENTAL".
- **Talking about the game is always fine.** Reviews, videos, streams, mods,
  wikis and fan art may use the name to refer to it.

The official paid release is (or will be) on Steam; buying it there supports
development.
